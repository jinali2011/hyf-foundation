 Quick Noodles

Ingredients: Noodles, 1 tbsp oil, garlic, onion, capsicum, carrot, soy sauce, chilli sauce, salt & pepper.

Steps:

Boil noodles and drain.
Heat oil; fry garlic and vegetables for 2–3 min.
Add noodles, 1–2 tbsp soy sauce, chilli sauce, salt & pepper.
Toss on high heat for 2–3 min.
Serve hot!
